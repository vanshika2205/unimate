A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you reach out.

How much does support or training cost?
Community groups, project briefs, and core outlines are open to all students. For 1-on-1 personalized mentorship or intensive workshops, scope and any applicable nominal fees are shared upfront before you decide. No charges are incurred by submitting an enquiry.
Can you complete my assignment for me?
No. Academic support focuses on concept explanations, record formatting guidance, viva question practice, and presentation framing so you can do your own work with integrity. We never write assessed papers or take tests for students.
Are internships paid?
We are completely transparent: core student contributor internships are typically 2-month unpaid remote learning stints focused on skill building, offer letters, and verifiable completion certificates. Any role offering a stipend will clearly state the terms upfront.
Who will my mentor be?
Mentors are experienced seniors, recent graduates, and practicing engineers/designers who have excelled in the exact skills you are trying to learn. Details and profiles are shared once we understand your goals.
Can I request a missing subject resource?
Yes! If you cannot find notes for your specific branch, elective, or semester, use the "Request a resource" form on our Academic Support page or post in our WhatsApp community branch circle.
Will an enquiry reserve a place?
No. Enquiries and expressions of interest are initial conversations. Formal registration, mentorship matching, or cohort selection is finalized directly with you afterwards.
